Ensure that a systemic challenge is chosen
When defining the scope of the project, think of Goldilocks and the porridge.

*A good MTS project*
>It's systemic or "wicked" problem - solutions are not clear
>
>Impactful- progress here would make a big and sustained difference
>Be passionate about the topic
>
>Scope the topic by geography, sector, or other parameter to make our work meaningful and feasible
>
>Can apply lived/ learnt experience to the topic
>
>Ready to lead with humility and have fun

*Common pitfalls when mapping a system includes*
>Scope of the problem is too large
>
>Going in with a set solution
>
>Only interviewing experts(knowledge equity)
>
>Forgetting about power dynamics
