**Alkyls**
	**Alkanes**- no functional group
	**Alkenes**- containing a carbon-carbon double bond
	**Alkynes**- containing a carbon-carbon triple bond

**Alcohols**- containing a hydroxyl group
**Ethers**- contains an alkoxy group(oxygen bonded to two alkyl groups)
**Amines**- contains a amino group(NH_2)
**Imines**- contains a nitrogen double bonded to a carbon(R-CHNH/R-CNH-R*)
**Nitro** Compounds- contains the nitro group(NO_2)
**Alkyl** **Halides**- contains a halide(F,Cl,Br,I)

**Carbonyls**- containing oxygen double bonded to carbon 
	**Aldehydes**- containing carbonyl a secondary carbon(R-CHO)
	**Ketones**- containing carbonyl bonded to two alkyl chain(R-CO-R*)
	**Carboxylic** **Acids**- containing a carbonyl which is in turn bound to a hydroxyl group and alkyl chain(R-COOH)
	**Esters**- containing a carboxyl group bound to another alkyl chain(R-COO-R*)
	**Amides**- containing a carbonyl group bound to an amino group(R-CONH_2) note: the nitrogen can be substituted with more alkyl groups instead of hydrogens
	**Nitriles**- containing carbon triple bonded to nitrogen(R-CN)
	**Acyl** **Chloride**- containing carboxyl group with the hydroxyl group replaced by chloride(R-COCl)
	**Acetals**- containing two single bonded oxygen atoms on one carbon(R-CHOO/R-COO-R*)
