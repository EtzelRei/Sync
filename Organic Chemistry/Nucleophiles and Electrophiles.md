**Nucleophiles**- groups of molecules with high energy(reactive) HOMOs; these molecules typically carry a negative formal charge and/or a lone pair. (Electron Donors/ Seeking nucleus)
>[!Examples of Nucleophiles]

**Electrophiles**- groups of molecules with a low energy(can be populated with electrons) LUMO. these molecules typically carry a positive formal charge.(Electron Acceptor/ Seeking Electrons)

>[!Possible Electrophiles]
Positively charged ions
Atoms next to electronegative/ electron withdrawing groups
>
>think carbocation, ammonium, hydronium, ketonium





Alkenes often can act as both a nucleophile and electrophile
